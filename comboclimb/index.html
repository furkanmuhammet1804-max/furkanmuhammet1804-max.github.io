FAQ

How do I play?

Tap the left/right side of the screen to run and tap to jump. Build speed and bounce off walls to skip floors and grow your combo chain. The game works offline.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up or login. Leaderboards only use a nickname you choose.

Why can't I see leaderboards / the Daily Tower?

Online modes need an internet connection and a one-time acceptance of the Terms of Use. If you choose "Not now", the game stays offline and nothing is published.

How do I report or block a player?

On the RANKING screen tap the flag icon on a player's row and choose "Report"; tap ⊘ to block them (blocked players are hidden from you). Every report is reviewed within 24 hours; offending content is removed and the player is banned.

Ads and privacy

The game is free and shows ads (Google AdMob). In the EU/UK you can change your ad choices in Settings → "Ad privacy choices".

How do I delete my data?

Email the address above with the subject "Combo Climb data deletion", your nickname and your friend code (shown in Settings). Within 30 days we delete all scores, the nickname and friend records stored for that install on our server. Deleting the app removes all local data on your device.
